Offer notes

The Latest tab reads one Markdown note per Discovery offer. A person can write them; an AI agent can write them from Discovery's emails, its Latest news pages and posts you paste in. The plugin only reads the notes, checks each one against your household, and sorts them.

The folder

Set it under Inputs → Latest offers → Offers folder (the default is Discovery offers). Every note under it, in any subfolder, that has discovery: offer in its frontmatter is an offer. Other notes there (an index, say) are left alone.

Inside it, Inbox/ is for emails, PDFs and screenshots not read yet. The tab shows how many files wait there. Whoever reads a file moves it to Inbox/Done/, which is not counted.

A word not on this list
Any other word (new-home-loan, say) cannot be checked, so the offer shows as Not sure who and Details names the word. That is on purpose: the tab never claims you have something it cannot see.

Who can use it

You are checked from the plugin's own answers: your products, your bank package and tier, your Vitality programmes and statuses, your Insure plan and home cover.

The others at home are typed under Inputs, each with their own Discovery Bank account and whether they are on the medical aid. Medical aid words follow yours for someone on it. The cars, the home and life cover count as the household's, so those words follow yours for everyone. A Vitality Money status or programme is not asked for others, so it shows as not known for them.

One person's requirement not met counts over one not known. An offer goes under Not for us only when nobody at home meets it, and the card says what is missing.

Rules for an agent writing the notes

  1. Only what the source says. Every figure, date, partner and condition comes from the email, page or post. When the source gives no end date, leave ends out and put "End date not given" in condition. Never make up a date.
  2. One offer, one note. Search the folder by title and partner before writing. The same offer in a second email updates the note (and adds to source); it is not a new note. Keep a state someone set.
  3. requires from the eligibility line. "Credit Card Account or Suite clients" is credit-card; "Transaction Account with bundled fees, Credit Card Account and Suite" is transaction-bundled | credit-card; "with your Ðiscovery Miles" is vitality-money. When the source names no eligibility, leave requires empty.
  4. Facts, not advice. benefit, condition and how describe the offer in Discovery's terms. They never say whether to take it up.
  5. Done with a file: move it from Inbox/ to Inbox/Done/.
